The referral pathway

DermAssist sits between the first examination and the specialist, so the dermatologist's limited time goes to the lesions most likely to be malignant.

  1. Capture

    A health assistant examines the patient and takes a dermoscopic image at the outpatient clinic.

  2. Triage

    DermAssist returns class probabilities, Grad-CAM and a refer or routine suggestion within seconds.

  3. Queue and book

    Referred cases join the review queue and can be booked into the next visiting clinic.

  4. Specialist review

    The dermatologist examines the patient and decides on biopsy, treatment or follow-up.
