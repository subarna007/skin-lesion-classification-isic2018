Checking the model…

Before you analyse

  • Use a dermoscopic image, taken through a dermatoscope, centred on one lesion.
  • The model has only seen dermoscopic images. A phone photo or a non-skin image still gets an answer, and that answer is meaningless.
  • The result supports a clinician's judgement and is not a diagnosis.

ABCDE rule

The clinical checklist for suspicious pigmented lesions. Asymmetry, border and colour are measured automatically on the outlined lesion; diameter and evolution need information a single photo does not contain, so the clinician enters them. These measurements are separate from the model's prediction and are not clinically validated.
